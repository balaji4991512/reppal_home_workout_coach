# RepPal privacy policy

Updated 27 October 2026

## In short
RepPal keeps your training data on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold. Some features use Google services, explained below.

## What is stored on your phone
Your setup (name if you give one, equipment, level, days, split, anything you asked to work around, height), your plan and progress for each exercise, your workouts with every set, any notes you typed or said, your weigh-ins, your habits with what you ticked or entered for each day, your planner tasks, your routines with the days you finished them, your journal, your goals, your focus sessions, and your weekly reflections. It stays in the app's private storage on this phone.

## Signing in with Google
Signing in is optional. If you choose it, Google confirms your account through Google Play services and Firebase Authentication, and RepPal receives your name and email address. Google keeps a record of the sign-in under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Your workouts are not uploaded: they stay on this phone either way.

## Voice input
The microphone is only used while you tap Mic during a workout. Your phone's speech service turns what you say into text: on the phone when it can, otherwise through Google's speech service online, under Google's privacy policy. RepPal keeps only the text you save with a set, never the audio.

## Bluetooth scale
If you weigh in with a Bluetooth scale, RepPal listens for the readings the scale broadcasts. It does not connect to the scale and does not use Bluetooth to find your location. It saves the weight, the time and an ID for your scale, so other people's scales nearby are ignored.

## Your choices
Profile lets you export everything to a file you keep, restore from such a file, and erase everything on this phone. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data from this phone. You can sign out at any time from the menu, and turn off microphone or Nearby devices access in Android settings.

## Deleting your account
If you signed in, Profile has Delete account. It deletes your account (your Google sign-in with RepPal, kept by Firebase) and erases everything on this phone. Without the app, you can ask for deletion at [contact email to be added before publishing].

## Health
RepPal is a training log, not medical advice. If an exercise hurts, stop and check with a doctor or physio.

## Children
RepPal is not meant for children under 13.
